Terms

These terms explain how to use the beta and what to expect from its resume tools.

Updated October 16, 2026

Using the app

Resume Prospective is a software product of PNB Paws LLC. Use your own emailed sign-in code and assigned access. Access passes may expire, and features may change while we improve the app. Do not share access codes, use another person’s account, or interfere with the service.

What you upload

Upload only material you have the right to use. Coaches must have the resume owner’s permission to upload their information. Keep resumes and job details accurate, and leave out patient identifiers or confidential information that does not belong in an application.

Review your result

Scores, suggestions, and AI drafts are guidance, not a certification of accuracy or a hiring decision. Check each name, date, title, credential, skill, number, and link before sending a resume. You choose the finished version. PNB Paws cannot promise an interview, offer, or hiring outcome.

Email and human help

The app emails a finished PDF only when delivery is selected and confirmed. Human resume help is optional and shares the PDF and Word versions with the configured review recipients when requested. Read Privacy for details about processing and storage.

Questions

For help with individual access, a result, or these terms, email owner@example.com. You can also read our Cookies page.
