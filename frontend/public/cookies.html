The little details

Cookies

We keep this simple: the app needs a session cookie to remember that you signed in.

Updated October 16, 2026

Essential session cookie

JSESSIONID connects your browser to the app’s server session for sign-in, access checks, and security. Coach and beta sessions expire after 15 minutes of inactivity; the admin session has its own limit. Signing out ends the current server session.

Other website connections

The app does not currently set advertising or analytics cookies. Its pages may load Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google to display those fonts. The fonts are not needed to sign in or use the resume tools.

Your browser controls

You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the essential session cookie may prevent sign-in. Read Privacy for how resume information is handled, or email owner@example.com with a question.
