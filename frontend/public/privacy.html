Privacy

Here is what happens to the information you share while using Resume Prospective.

Updated October 17, 2026

What we use

We use the email you enter to send sign-in codes, deliver a resume when requested, and respond to help requests. The app processes the resume, optional job posting, and answers you provide to preview, rate, and build resumes. It also keeps sign-in, coach-assignment, and pass records needed to run the app.

AI review and notifications

Resume text and, when needed, page images are sent to our configured AI provider for review and drafting. PNB Paws receives an email notice when a coach, mentor, or user signs in. A rating sends a notice with the resume owner's entered email, any coach email, career level, and scores. Starting the builder sends a separate notice. In the coach and individual versions, PNB Paws also receives the uploaded resume and AI feedback PDF, followed by draft and finished PDF and Word copies if a resume is built.

Sharing and storage

Resume and optional job-posting content goes to our configured OpenAI API for review and drafting. Our hosting provider runs the app, and our email provider handles sign-in codes, alerts, and requested deliveries. A user’s finished PDF goes to their sign-in address; a coach can send it to the resume owner’s email entered for that review. Human-help recipients get the PDF and Word files only if that help is requested.

Accounts and free trials

We store your email, verification and approval status, password hash if you create a password, and trial usage. One free review and build is available per email across versions. Your trial email and usage remain recorded to prevent repeat trials. We save trial review and build results so you can return to them. The owner receives a notice when your trial is used and when you request an account or subscription. Passwords are stored as hashes, not readable passwords.

How long it remains

The app keeps access and pass records to operate the app. A Word file shared through a private human-help link expires after seven days and is removed from that link’s storage. Emails and attachments may remain in senders’ and recipients’ mailboxes under those services’ retention settings. Clearing an app session does not remove emails already sent.

Your choices and questions

You can stop before submitting a resume, choose whether to request human help, and clear your current app session. Please do not upload patient identifiers or information you are not authorized to share. To ask about your information, correction, or deletion, email owner@example.com. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

Saved progress

When you choose Save progress, your uploaded file, resume draft, answers, and current step are saved to your account so you can continue later. A new save replaces your previous saved progress. It remains through app updates and is removed when your account is deleted.
