A little help before you start

Frequently asked questions

Your resume should sound like you. Here are a few things to know before we get to work.

What does Resume Prospective actually do?

It reviews your resume, explains what needs work, and helps you build a clearer version. You can edit the wording, compare your version with the PNB Paws version, and choose the one you want to use.

Who is this for?

Job seekers, career coaches, and mentors. You do not have to be a software engineer to use it. Choose the field that fits your work, including general careers, healthcare, education, or software engineering.

What does my score mean?

A score is feedback on your resume, not a rating of you. The app looks at presentation and content so you can see what is working and what needs attention. A 10/10 is not a promise of an interview or a job. Use the feedback to make your next draft stronger.

Will AI make up experience for me?

That is not what this is for. Your resume needs to reflect what you have actually done. The app is designed to work from the details you provide, without made-up jobs, skills, degrees, or results. AI can still get things wrong, so check the names, dates, numbers, and links before you use the finished resume.

Can I edit my resume and download it?

Yes. Edit your sections, compare the two versions, and choose your finished resume. You can download a PDF or Word document. If you choose email delivery, check the recipient and confirm it before sending.

Who receives my resume and information?

The app uses OpenAI to help review and draft your resume. PNB Paws also receives activity notices and, in the coach and individual versions, uploaded, draft, and finished resume copies. Email delivery and human help share files as described on our Privacy page. Please read it before uploading, and only share information you have permission to use.
